Welcome to Our Space

Where Every Bite Carries
a Story & a Soul

Founded over 35 years ago, Hinglaj Farsan was born out of a deep love for India's vibrant street-food culture. From our signature crispy chakli and melt-in-your-mouth mohanthal to piping-hot samosas and refreshing chaas, every recipe has been lovingly passed down through generations. We believe great food is not just sustenance — it is a tradition, a memory, and a moment of pure joy.
